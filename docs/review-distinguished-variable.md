# Distinguished-variable prime relation: review record

## Frozen scope

The reviewed slice is
`AlgebraicAnalysis/Polynomial/DistinguishedVariable.lean`. It proves that a
homogeneous polynomial differs from its pure distinguished monomial by an
element of the ideal generated by its other allowed variables, and derives the
corresponding prime-ideal membership statement when the pure coefficient is a
unit.

## Independent review

An independent mathematical audit returned `REPAIR` on the pre-integration
state because the required scheme-level congruence had not yet been present.
It confirmed the proposed implication and identified the exact missing bridge
as

```text
P - c X_t^N in span {X_i | i auxiliary}.
```

The implemented theorem is precisely that bridge. A separate Lean prototype
and consumer compiled with `--trust=0` and found the same Mathlib route:
`mem_ideal_span_X_image`, removal of the unit coefficient, and prime-power
extraction.

## Verification and boundary

The package build, API test, and direct `--trust=0` compilation pass. The
reported axioms are only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`.
This slice proves no filtered strictness, associated-graded coordinate
surjectivity, or D-module noncharacteristic theorem. Those remain obligations
of downstream consumers.
